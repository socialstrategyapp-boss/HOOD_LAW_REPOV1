# HOOD-LAW CHAMBERS

HOOD-LAW CHAMBERS is a modular Australian criminal litigation operating system for New South Wales criminal defence practice, appellate advocacy, evidence analysis, sentencing strategy, legal knowledge retrieval, and forensic legal reasoning.

This repository is not a generic legal app. It is the foundation for a permanent digital chambers architecture built for:

- criminal defence barristers
- appellate counsel
- sentencing specialists
- evidence-law strategists
- legal research operators

## Core jurisdictions

- New South Wales
- Commonwealth criminal law
- NSW Court of Criminal Appeal practice
- High Court authority
- Evidence Act 1995 (NSW)
- Crimes (Sentencing Procedure) Act 1999 (NSW)
- Criminal Procedure Act 1986 (NSW)
- Drug Misuse and Trafficking Act 1985 (NSW)
- Bail Act 2013 (NSW)

## Operating principles

The system must be designed for:

- precision
- security
- scalability
- auditability
- modularity
- long-term knowledge retention

## AI and legal accuracy guardrails

Any future automation in this repository must:

- never generate fake authorities
- never hallucinate citations
- distinguish facts from inference
- identify uncertainty clearly
- preserve legal nuance
- prioritise legal accuracy over speed

## Core chambers modules

1. **Chambers library** — structured authority storage, ratio extraction, principle tagging, and related-case linking.
2. **Case extraction engine** — judgment summaries, favourable/unfavourable passage extraction, and sentencing-comparison intelligence.
3. **Evidence warfare module** — admissibility analysis, objection workflows, unlawful-search review, and s 138 exclusion support.
4. **Cross-examination system** — witness analysis, contradiction mapping, sequence locking, and credibility testing.
5. **Appeal-ground generator** — House error, manifest excess, procedural unfairness, and other appellate-vulnerability analysis.
6. **Sentencing database** — offence categories, sentencing ranges, plea discounts, parity, totality, and comparative matrices.
7. **Judicial profile engine** — judicial tendencies, evidentiary strictness, rehabilitation weighting, deterrence weighting, and survivability indicators.
8. **Digital and federal crime division** — telecommunications evidence, device extraction analysis, cloud evidence, crypto tracing awareness, and financial-analysis support.
9. **Operations manual** — brief analysis, chronology building, hearing preparation, appeal preservation, and conference workflow automation.

## Initial repository layout

The repository now uses a documented top-level structure so future development stays modular and scalable:

```text
.
├── apps/         # Next.js and React user-facing applications
├── database/     # PostgreSQL schema, migrations, seed data, audit models
├── docs/         # Architecture, standards, and domain design documentation
├── knowledge/    # Markdown authorities, legal principles, and research artefacts
├── operations/   # Practice workflows, playbooks, and hearing-preparation materials
└── services/     # Python/TypeScript APIs and domain engines
```
